# Accessibility in PetCompanion

Created by Rosetta.

PetCompanion aims to be a comfortable companion for people with different ways of using a computer. Accessibility feedback is welcome, including from people using keyboards, screen readers, magnification or reduced-motion settings.

## Current scope

The published app is for Windows x64. It offers a full panel and compact mode. Expand compact mode when you need the full set of controls, and use the app's settings for available effects options.

Accessibility is ongoing work. This statement does not claim formal accessibility certification or complete compatibility with every assistive technology. Pet artwork, animations and changing color palettes need testing alongside the app's controls and text.

## Report a barrier

Use [Accessibility feedback](https://github.com/MagicJocey/petcompanion-releases/issues/new?template=03-accessibility.yml). Tell us:

- Your app version and Windows version.
- The task you were trying to complete.
- What prevented or made that task difficult.
- Whether you were using full or compact mode.
- Any relevant keyboard, screen-reader, zoom, contrast or motion settings, if you are comfortable sharing them.
- What change would help.

You do not need to share a diagnosis, disability history or personal notebook content. Screenshots and short recordings are optional; remove private information before attaching them.

Questions and general feedback can also go in [Discussions](https://github.com/MagicJocey/petcompanion-releases/discussions).
